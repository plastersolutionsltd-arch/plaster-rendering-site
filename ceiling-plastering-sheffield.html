About Our Service

Ceiling Plastering Sheffield — Skimming, Crack Repairs, Artex & Reboarding

P&R Solutions plasters and repairs ceilings in homes across Sheffield, every postcode from S1 to S36. A ceiling takes more abuse than any wall: it moves with the joists above it, it catches every leak from a bath or a roof, and in a lot of Sheffield houses it is still carrying a textured coating from the 1970s or 80s. When it starts to crack, sag or stain, painting over it only buys a few months.

Most ceilings fall into one of three jobs. If the ceiling is sound and just cracked or tired, we tape the cracks and apply a two-coat finish skim over the whole surface. If it is sagging, water-damaged or loose, we take it down or fix new plasterboard over it and skim the new board. If it is artex, we either skim over it or seal it under new board, after it has been tested where the house dates from before 2000.

We say which of the three your ceiling needs at the survey, and why. A skim over a ceiling that is coming away from the joists is wasted money, and so is a full reboard of a ceiling that only needed its cracks taping. You get the cheaper option whenever it will last.

Ceilings are indoor work, so we plaster them all through the winter. Furniture is moved or sheeted, floors are covered, and the room is left clean at the end of each day. A standard bedroom or living room ceiling is usually finished in a day.

Every ceiling is covered by our 5-year workmanship guarantee and our no-deposit policy: you pay when the work is done and you are happy with it. See our Sheffield plastering prices, call 07595 399525 or request a quote online.

Ceiling Problems We Fix

Cracked. Sagging. Artex.

Most ceiling problems in Sheffield homes come down to one of five things. Each needs a different fix, and the right one is often cheaper than people expect.

Get a Quote ↗
Cracks Along the Board Joints ›

Fine straight cracks, often in a grid, are the joints between the plasterboards showing through. They come back if you only fill them. We scrim-tape every crack and joint, then skim the whole ceiling so the repair cannot telegraph through the paint.

Sagging or Bowed Ceilings ›

A ceiling that dips, bulges or has come away from the joists is not safe to skim. We either take the old ceiling down and board to the joists, or fix new plasterboard straight over it into the joists, then tape and skim. We will tell you which at the survey.

Artex and Textured Ceilings ›

Artex can usually be skimmed over or sealed under new plasterboard without scraping it off. If your house was built or decorated before 2000, the artex is tested for asbestos before anything is sanded or disturbed. Overboarding seals it in completely.

Water Stains and Leak Damage ›

Once a leak is fixed, a stained ceiling that is still firm can be sealed and skimmed. Board that has gone soft, crumbly or brown right through is cut out and replaced with new board before the skim, so the stain never comes back through.

Patched and Uneven After Rewiring ›

New downlights, rewires and plumbing leave ceilings full of patches at different levels. A full skim brings the whole ceiling back to one flat, even surface, ready for a single coat of mist paint.

How We Work

How We Plaster Your Ceiling.

High Green S35: artex ceilings removed, reboarded and skimmed.

A ceiling is plastered in five stages. The preparation decides whether the finish lasts.

Book a Survey ↗
1

Survey & Diagnosis

We check whether the ceiling is sound, find the cause of any cracking or staining, and decide between a skim, an overboard or new board. Pre-2000 artex is tested before work starts.

2

Protect the Room

Furniture moved out or sheeted, floors covered, doorways screened. Light fittings are taken down by your electrician or made safe before we start.

3

Repair or Reboard

Cracks and joints scrim-taped. Loose or damaged board cut out, or new plasterboard screwed into the joists, with noggings where the board edges need support.

4

Skim Finish

Two coats of finish plaster trowelled to a smooth, flat surface across the whole ceiling, so no repair shows through.

5

Dry & Hand Over

Most ceilings dry in 2 to 4 days, longer in a cold room. We walk you round the finish before you pay. 5-year guarantee. Zero deposit.

FAQ

Common Questions About Ceilings Answered.

What Sheffield homeowners ask us about cracked, sagging and artex ceilings. Click any question for the full answer.

01Pricing
How much does it cost to plaster a ceiling in Sheffield?

As a guide, skimming the ceiling of a standard room costs about £150 to £280, and fixing new plasterboard and skimming it costs about £250 to £350. Artex ceilings cost about £10 to £14 per m² to skim over, or £20 to £30 per m² to overboard and skim. We give a free, itemised quote after a survey.

+
02Cracks
Can you plaster over a cracked ceiling?

Yes, if the ceiling is still firmly fixed to the joists. Every crack is scrim-taped first, then the whole ceiling is skimmed so the cracks cannot show through again. If the ceiling has dropped or feels loose, it needs new plasterboard instead, and we will tell you that at the survey.

+
03Artex
Can you skim over artex?

Usually, yes. Well-bonded artex can be skimmed over, or sealed under new plasterboard and skimmed, without scraping it off. If the house was built or decorated before 2000, the artex is tested for asbestos first, because HSE guidance says textured coatings from that period can contain it.

+
04Artex
Does my artex ceiling contain asbestos?

It might. HSE guidance is that asbestos can be present in any building built or refurbished before 2000, and textured coatings like artex are one of the common places it turns up. The only way to know is to have a sample tested. Overboarding seals artex in without disturbing it.

+
05Method
Should I overboard or replaster my ceiling?

Overboarding means fixing new plasterboard over the old ceiling and skimming it. It is quicker and cleaner, and suits artex or badly patched ceilings that are still secure. If the old ceiling is sagging, wet or falling away, it comes down first and new board goes up to the joists.

+
06Timing
How long does ceiling plastering take?

A standard bedroom or living room ceiling is usually skimmed in a day. Overboarding or replacing a ceiling usually adds a day. The plaster then needs 2 to 4 days to dry, longer in a cold room, before a mist coat of paint.

+
07Mess
Is ceiling plastering messy, and do I need to empty the room?

Plastering is wet work, not dusty work, unless old plaster is coming down. We cover floors, sheet anything left in the room and clean up at the end of each day. Taking smaller items out makes the job quicker; larger furniture can be moved to the middle and covered.

+
08Winter
Can you plaster ceilings in winter?

Yes. Ceilings are indoor work, so we plaster them all year. In cold weather the room needs some gentle heat while the plaster dries, but not blasting heaters, which can make it crack. We will tell you how to treat the room afterwards.
