# Master NAP block — paste this, identically, into every listing

**The whole point of this file is that every directory gets the SAME text.** A citation works by
independent sources agreeing with each other. "Grenoside" on one and "Sheffield" on another, or
"07595 399525" on one and "+44 7595 399525" on another, splits the evidence and wastes the listing.
Copy from here. Do not retype from memory.

Every value below was read from the site's own LocalBusiness schema on 8 Sep 2026, not from notes.

```
Business name:   Plaster and Rendering Solutions Limited

Email:           plastersolutionsltd@gmail.com
Website:         https://www.plasterandrenderingsolutions.co.uk/
```

**Name:** always the full `Plaster and Rendering Solutions Limited`. Never "P&R Solutions",
never "Plaster & Rendering Solutions" with an ampersand, never drop "Limited". The site uses the
full form in 143 places and the GBP uses it too — match it exactly.

**Phone formats.** Use whichever the form asks for, but only these two:
- Display / UK forms: `07595 399525`
- International / schema fields: `+447595399525`

**Address line 2 is "Grenoside" and it matters.** It is the suburb the business is actually in,
it is where the Map Pack measures distance from, and `rendering grenoside` is a keyword you hold
at position 1. Do not flatten it to just "Sheffield".

## Descriptions — three lengths, pick by the field limit

Every one of these carries the **service and the area**, because that is what a citation is for.
Do not write a fresh one on the spot; that is how inconsistency creeps in.

**Short (150 chars) — for listings with a tight limit:**
```
Plastering and rendering specialists in Sheffield. Silicone render, monocouche, EWI and internal
plastering. Free survey, no deposit, 5-year guarantee.
```

**Medium (approx 300 chars) — the one most directories want:**
```
Plaster and Rendering Solutions Limited are plastering and rendering specialists based in
Grenoside, Sheffield, covering the whole city and surrounding South Yorkshire. Silicone render,
monocouche render, external wall insulation, render repairs, internal plastering and dry lining.
Over 25 years' experience, NVQ-qualified and CHAS accredited, with 100+ five-star Google reviews.
Free local survey, no deposit taken, 5-year guarantee.
```

**Long (approx 600 chars) — where there is room:**
```
Plaster and Rendering Solutions Limited are plastering and rendering specialists based in
Grenoside, Sheffield, working across the city and the surrounding South Yorkshire area.

We cover silicone render, monocouche render, external wall insulation (EWI), sand and cement
render, render repairs, internal plastering (float and skim) and dry lining and plasterboarding.
We work with K-Rend, Eco-Rend, Johnstone's and Weber systems, and we can spray through the winter
using a cold-weather render rated down to -5C, when most renderers stop below +5C.

Over 25 years' experience. NVQ-qualified, CHAS accredited, 100+ five-star Google reviews.
Free local survey, no deposit taken, and a 5-year guarantee on completed work.
```

## Honest weighting — read this before spending a weekend on it

Citations are **7%** of local ranking weight. Reviews are **16%** and the Google Business Profile
is **32%**. This list is worth working through because it is free and one-off, and because the
NAP consistency it buys is permanent. It is **not** the thing that will move position on its own.
Reviews toward ~150 and the GBP remain the bigger levers. See `seo-ranking-tracker.md`.
